SUPPLEMENTARY DATA FILES

of the article "Conjugate Heat Transfer Analysis of Single-Phase Immersion Cooling for an
18650 Cell at Peak Discharge: Anisotropic Interface Coupling Pitfalls and Low-Cost Cloud
Execution" by A. R. Lakshmipathi and V. Dhanapathy.

These files belong to the Supplementary Material (supplement.pdf).  They hold the result
tables of all runs and the list of the numbers that the article and the Supplementary
Material quote, each with its source.  Supplementary Note S11 of supplement.pdf (the run
index) relates the identifiers used here to the names used in the article.  Section 5 of
this file says in which table each group of runs is found and names the trial and stopped
runs from which no result was taken.

File and folder names are those of the working repository of the project, so that a path
quoted in a table note of the Supplementary Material is also the path of the file here.
Some file and column names differ from the working repository; section 6 lists them.  Case
files, generators, analysis scripts and the library source are not in this folder; they are
publicly available at https://github.com/raman3366/openfoam-cht-18650-immersion .

All files are plain text: tables with comma-separated values (.csv) and text files (.txt).


1. THE LIST OF NUMBERS

numbers_quoted.csv
    One row for every number that the article or the Supplementary Material quotes from a
    run, a mesh, a dictionary or the cost meter.  A number quoted with different rounding in
    different places has one row per printed form.  The list is written by a script from
    the sources of the two documents.  Columns:

    key                    identifier of the number (see "Keys" below)
    kind_of_source         table of numbers | derived number of a text block |
                           file and line | arithmetic
    printed_value          the value as printed
    source_value           the value in its source, unrounded and in the unit of the source
                           (the printed value may be in another unit, for example ms for s,
                           mm/s for m/s or degC for K); for "file and line" the number on
                           the cited line that is closest to the printed value; empty for
                           "arithmetic"
    unit                   unit of source_value
    definition             what the number is
    source_file            file of the project from which the value was read (a file under
                           runs/ is in a run directory, which is not part of this folder)
    quoted_in              article, supplement, or both
    fragments_and_number_of_uses
                           the parts of the two documents that print the number, with the
                           number of places: W1 to W7 are the parts of the article in the
                           order of its sections, S01 to S11 the Supplementary Notes

    Keys.  "topic.dataset.quantity" is an entry of the project's table of numbers.  The data
    set is the identifier of a run (Supplementary Note S11), two identifiers joined by
    "_minus_" for the difference between two runs, or a named data set.  The topics are:

    inp     inputs, geometry, meshes and constants derived from them; published values
            with which the inputs are compared
    fam     the four runs with the final settings and the two twins with half the time step
    chk     checks of the number of correctors, of the algorithm and of the time step
    gci     grid study: ratios of successive differences, classes, observed orders
    unc     indicator of the numerical uncertainty
    htc     heat-transfer coefficient: definitions and their differences
    en      energy account of each run
    mass    coolant mass that leaves through the pressure-reference cell
    pil     pilot runs on the coarse mesh
    fin     fine mesh: the discharge with three correctors and its seven restart pilots
    def     runs with the scalar option and face-by-face audit of the cell surface
            ("def" is a working name)
    port    runs and short tests with an opening at constant pressure
    cp      twin with constant coolant heat capacity
    sens    runs with one input changed
    plate   verification of the fluid solver on a heated vertical plate; coefficients of
            the cell runs by part of the cell surface
    bench   verification suite
    cost    time steps, run times, ranks, strong scaling and metered cost
    slow    slow oscillation of the plume above the cell late in the discharge
            (Supplementary Note S6, Part G)
    shift   difference of the temperatures between two runs that differ in the number of
            pressure correctors or in the time step, before and after the slow oscillation
            sets in (Supplementary Note S6, Part G)
    scan    two-step content of the velocity series sampled at each time step, scanned over
            the whole run in blocks of 2 s (Supplementary Note S6, Part F)
    body    numbers of the text of the article that belong to none of the topics above
    lim     the three discharges with one corrector and the velocity limiter (data sets
            limiter_coarse, limiter_medium and limiter_fine)

    A key "blocks:<letter>.<name>" is a number derived for the text of the Supplementary
    Notes (A coolant, B cell and heat source, C and M and P fixed volume, mass loss and
    expansion port, G and I reference times and headline values, L plate, S pilots, E and V
    single values).  A key "file:<path>:<line>" is read from that line of that file.  A key
    "calc:<expression>" is arithmetic on the entries it names, or a constant of a
    definition (a threshold, the limit of a time window).

    The definitions are those of the project's table of numbers.  They use some working
    names: "core" or "T_core" is the hot spot, "can" the cell surface, "fluid" the coolant,
    "solid" or "battery" the cell, "aniso" and "iso" the anisotropic region and its
    isotropic neighbor region, "Rule A1" the cost as a share of the occupied cores and
    "Rule A2" the cost with idle capacity spread over the runs.  Energies and heat rates of
    the cell model are for the whole cell and bath (the model is one quadrant) unless a
    definition says otherwise.


2. RESULT TABLES OF THE CELL RUNS

2.1 Discharges

results/r1_runs/familyC.csv
    The four discharges with the final settings (extra-coarse, coarse, medium and fine
    mesh) and the two twins with half the time step and half the Courant limit.

results/r1_runs/summary_2026-10-12.csv
    Nine discharges on the coarse and medium meshes that differ from the final settings in
    the pressure-velocity step (three or four pressure correctors; three outer passes with
    two correctors each), three of them with a changed input and one with the scalar
    option.

results/r1_runs/batch3.csv
    Eight further discharges with the final settings: expansion port (coarse, medium),
    constant coolant heat capacity, scalar option (coarse, medium), two other conductivity
    pairs and the raised heat rate; the coarse and medium runs with the final settings are
    repeated in it for comparison.

results/r1_runs/remaining_discharges.csv
    The three discharges of Supplementary Note S11 that have no row in the three tables
    above: D12 (medium mesh, three correctors, half the time step), D13 (fine mesh, three
    correctors, stopped at 110.9 s) and D17 (medium mesh, scalar option, three correctors).
    This table was written for this folder from the project's table of numbers (topics chk,
    fin, def, en and cost; the same entries are in numbers_quoted.csv where a note quotes
    them); the settings are those recorded in the run directory, and verdict and accept
    are the lines of the analysis script for the run.  Columns as in the tables above, with
    these differences: t_end is the time of the last time step (s); u_peak_200 and
    u_peak_end are the largest speed in the coolant at 200 s and at the last sample (m/s),
    in place of u_peak; E_gen_J and global_resid_pct are not given.  For D13 no sample at
    200 s or at 267.0 s exists; its note gives what was evaluated.

results/r1_runs/one_corrector_limiter.csv
    The three discharges with one pressure corrector and the velocity limiter that Section
    V-C of the article quotes (coarse, medium and fine mesh).  They are no part of the 26
    discharges of the tables above and of Supplementary Notes S10 and S11.

    Columns of the run tables.  run: identifier (Supplementary Note S11); mesh ("xcoarse"
    is the extra-coarse mesh); nCorr: pressure correctors per outer pass; nOuter: outer
    passes; maxCo: Courant limit; maxDt: time-step cap (s); T_core, T_mean, T_can, T_bath:
    hot spot, cell mean, cell-surface temperature and bath temperature at 267.0 s (degC);
    dT_radial: hot spot minus T_can (K); h_200, h_250: heat-transfer coefficient by the
    definition of the article at 200 and 250 s (W/(m2 K)); u_peak (m/s), E_gen_J (J) and
    global_resid_pct: see below; fluid_resid_pct: balance of the coolant as a percentage of
    the interface heat; verdict: result of the search for a two-step oscillation in the
    series sampled at every time step; accept: result of the acceptance tests of the
    analysis script; steps: time steps; note: remark.

    Two columns are NOT quantities of the article (Supplementary Note S11, Part D): u_peak
    is the largest speed in the coolant at the last sample of the run, not the peak speed at
    200 s of Table V; global_resid_pct is an estimate with constant properties and is not
    the remainder of the energy account of the article.  E_gen_J is the nominal heat rate
    of the run times the time span of its monitors.  On the two coarser meshes the plume
    above the cell oscillates late in the discharge (Supplementary Note S6, Part G), so
    that u_peak of a coarse-mesh run is one sample of an oscillating value.

results/r1_runs/familyC_gci.csv
    Grid study of the four runs with the final settings: for each quantity the value on
    the four meshes and, for the coarser and the finer mesh triplet, the ratio of successive
    differences (R), the class (kind), and the output of the analysis script for an order
    (p), an extrapolated value (ext) and an index (GCI%), followed by a least-squares fit
    over the four meshes (lsq_*).  The script fills p, ext and GCI% for every triplet that
    is not divergent.  The article assigns an observed order to monotone triplets only and
    uses no extrapolated value, no index and no least-squares fit; its indicator of the
    numerical uncertainty is three times the range over the four meshes (Section V-D,
    Supplementary Note S7).  The peak speed on the extra-coarse mesh is one sample of an
    oscillating value, so that R, kind and p of its coarser triplet depend on the sampling
    instant (Supplementary Note S7, Part B).

results/r1_pilots/stage1_pi_groupA.csv
results/r1_pilots/stage1_vm.csv

results/r1_pilots/fine_restart_pilots.csv
    The seven restart pilots on the fine mesh (restart table of Supplementary Note S6),
    one row each, unrounded.  This table was written for this folder from the project's
    table of numbers (topic fin).  Columns: number_in_note_S11 (R1 to R7); identifier;
    change; nOuter, nCorr, nNonOrth: outer passes, pressure correctors, non-orthogonal
    corrections; dt_cap_ms: time-step cap (ms); D: cell viscous number at the cap;
    t_restart_s: time of the state the pilot continues from; t_span_s: time covered;
    steps; amplitude_start_m_s, amplitude_end_m_s: two-step amplitude at the probe in the
    column below the cell on the first steps and as the median of the last 50 steps (m/s);
    factor_per_step: factor of the two-step amplitude per time step; verdict: result of
    the search of the analysis script; fluid_resid_pct: balance of the coolant as a
    percentage of the interface heat over the pilot; u_max_last_m_s: largest speed at the
    last step; wall_s, s_per_step: wall clock of the solver and per time step (s).

results/r1_pilots/fine_restart_pilots_summary.txt
    Output of the analysis script for the seven restart pilots, one block per pilot (the
    blocks are named c4, c5, dt3, o3c2, no2, dt15 and ref).  Line 8 of this file is a
    source of the list of numbers.  The lines "ACCEPTANCE" are the acceptance tests of the
    analysis script.  Every pilot fails the test of the lowest coolant temperature (16 mK
    below the initial temperature in the pilots; 17 mK in the discharge that they
    continue); the two pilots in which the two-step oscillation persists also fail the
    tests of that oscillation, and the pilot with three outer passes the test of the
    balance of the coolant (1.7 % against a limit of 1 %).  The restart table of
    Supplementary Note S6 uses the two-step amplitudes and the balance of the coolant.

results/r1_cloud/scaling_cx3d-176x440_2026-10-12.txt
    Strong-scaling measurement (Supplementary Note S10, Part E), one line per run: mesh,
    cells, MPI ranks, cells per rank, time steps, wall clock per time step (s), core-seconds
    per time step, exit status of the solver.

    Run time and metered cost of each of the 67 runs on the cloud machine (Tables S10.1 and
    S10.2 of Supplementary Note S10) are in numbers_quoted.csv, topic cost.

2.3 Interface audit of the cell

results/r1_addons/interface_audit/interface_split.csv
    Face-by-face audit of the cell surface (Supplementary Note S8) for five runs at 130 and
    260 s, by part of the surface.  "can wall" is the side wall, "top cap", "bottom cap" and
    "both caps" the end caps, "whole patch" the cell surface.  Qf is the coolant-side heat
    rate, Qs_tensor the cell-side tensor flux with its normal and tangential part,
    Qs_stock_scalar the cell-side value by the formula of the stock heat-flux object.
    Heat rates in this file are for the simulated quadrant, one quarter of the cell.

results/r1_addons/interface_audit/interface_decomposition.csv
    Split of the interface difference into the heat-capacity term and the staggering term,
    at 130 s, at 260 s and over the whole run, for three runs (Supplementary Note S8,
    Part D).  Values for the simulated quadrant.

results/r1_addons/interface_audit/energy_audit.csv
    Energy account over the discharge of the same five runs, D17 among them, one row per
    run (Supplementary Note S8, Part C).  Energies in J for the simulated quadrant between
    t_a and t_b: E_gen generated; dE_cell stored in the cell; E_s_rel released by the cell
    (E_gen - dE_cell); E_int_f received by the coolant through the cell surface;
    D_interface their difference; dE_bath change of the enthalpy of the coolant; E_wall
    heat through the outer walls (negative: leaving), with its parts by wall; E_dpdt,
    E_grav, dKE pressure work, gravity work and change of kinetic energy; M_a coolant mass
    at the start and dM its change (kg); E_mass enthalpy of the liquid that leaves through
    the pressure-reference cell; R_fluid_rhoh and R_fluid_open residual of the balance of
    the coolant without and with that enthalpy; pct_gen_*: shares of E_gen (%);
    D_pct_of_E_int_f and R_open_pct_of_E_int_f: shares of E_int_f (%).  dE_bath, E_mass,
    R_fluid_rhoh, global_unaccounted and the shares formed from them are counted from the
    temperature from which the solver counts enthalpy (298.15 K) and are not quantities of
    the article; D_interface and R_fluid_open do not depend on that temperature.

results/r1_addons/interface_audit/twins_three_correctors.csv
    The same account for the coarse- and medium-mesh discharges with three pressure
    correctors and the directional option (D7 and D11), each beside its twin with five
    correctors.

6. HOW THE FILES DIFFER FROM THOSE OF THE WORKING REPOSITORY

No number was changed.  The copied tables are copies of the files of the same path in the
working repository, with the following differences.

Names.  Some file and column names differ from the working repository:
results/r1_runs/one_corrector_limiter.csv and
results/r1_addons/expansion_port/short_tests.csv have other names there, and the column
cont_cum_reference_cell of the latter another name.  In numbers_quoted.csv one identifier
of the table of numbers is renamed (cost.campaign.metered_hours); the topic of the three
discharges with one corrector and the limiter is written "lim" and its data sets
limiter_coarse, limiter_medium and limiter_fine, in keys, definitions and sources (the
Supplementary Notes name them so); their run directory is written
runs/one_corrector_limiter/;
a path that begins with paper/ lies one folder level deeper in the working repository; a
source outside the repository is given by a description in place of its path, and the host
name of the cloud machine by the words "the cloud machine".

Labels.  In free-text labels and remarks of the run tables and of the pilot tables, in the
summary of the restart pilots and in one line of results_columns.txt, working names of the
project were replaced by the names used in the article (for example "final settings",
"reference inputs", "scalar option", "three pressure correctors").  Two remarks of the run
tables were brought in line with Supplementary Notes S2 and S9: the conductivity pair
